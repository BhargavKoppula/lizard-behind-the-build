# 5. Building Lizard v2

“Lizard v2” is the project's name for a development phase, not the version number of today's Chrome package. The published package is 3.3.1. Keeping those labels separate prevents a product-history milestone from being mistaken for a Store release.

The v2 direction moved beyond a one-screen focus timer. Session continuity, more useful history and a less simplistic account of activity became design priorities. The available notes place this phase in September 2026, but do not establish one exact transition date or preserve a complete list of decisions from that moment.

The problem underneath the work was bigger than a clock that keeps running: people expect a session to be understandable when they return to it, and they need to see what the product actually observed rather than a confident but unsupported “focus score.” This journal records those goals without publishing the private recovery design or implementation details.

In the following month, the session model broadened into Work, Reading and Video. The three categories express different contexts for reflection; they are not separate psychological measurements. A Video session can provide a limited view of compatible player behavior, but playback alone cannot show that a person watched or learned.

“v2” is therefore best read as a product-thinking chapter: a shift from timing a block to describing a session cautiously. The exact boundary between that chapter and later development remains approximate in the retained records.
