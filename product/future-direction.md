# Future direction

This page distinguishes the current experience from questions the project may explore. Research possibilities are not product commitments or completed capabilities.

## Current product

The published Chrome Web Store package is version 3.3.1. Work, Reading and Video sessions, timeline/history views, simple patterns, exports, notifications and optional Lizzy are part of the current user-facing experience. See [features and experience](features-and-experience.md).

## Open verification

Retained browser evidence leaves Video playback accounting and some lifecycle/site scenarios incompletely verified. The project should describe their status accurately and gather reproducible, privacy-safe evidence before making broader claims. These are verification needs, not claims that a fix is underway.

## Research possibilities

- Explore interaction density and session consistency as descriptive summaries.
- Ask how reading and video session patterns might be represented without inferring comprehension or learning.
- Investigate how sample size and uncertainty should be communicated.
- Evaluate any proposed measure against a clearly defined outcome and suitable ground truth.
- Consider privacy-preserving ways to summarize behavior while collecting and retaining less.

Any such work would need explicit definitions, careful consent, privacy review and evidence appropriate to the claim. Until then, Lizard remains an experiment in personal reflection, not an attention-measurement system.
